Route in words
  1. Start at Dalsetta car park. Toilets are next to the car park.
  2. Follow the fire trail north-east for 1.2 km. Water tank at 1.1 km.
  3. Turn left onto the signed Gibraltar Peak track. It climbs steadily, with some steps.
  4. From 1.6 km to 3.1 km the track climbs at 10% or more, up to 14%. Granite tors at 3.6 km.
  5. Reach the summit lookout at 4.1 km. Return the same way.

Along the way: parking and toilets at the start, a water tank at 1.1 km, a photo spot at 2.9 km and the lookout at 4.1 km. Points of interest are from OpenStreetMap, within 200 m of the route.

About this trail

An out and back walk of 8.2 km from Dalsetta car park to the granite summit of Gibraltar Peak, climbing 520 m. The first 1.2 km follows a gravel fire trail; the rest is a dirt track that climbs at 10% or more for 1.5 km, up to 14%. Along the way it passes a water tank, granite tors and a lookout at the summit. The nearest town is Tharwa, 16 km away.
