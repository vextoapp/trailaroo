Two directions for the same parts: a trail card, filter chips, the Record stats, the difficulty legend and a fire-danger notice. Each is shown in dark and light mode. Chosen 23 Nov 2026: A, Eucalyptus. B is kept for the record and shows in the system font.

A. Eucalyptus

Blue-green of eucalypt leaves on deep bush green. Calm, and it stays clear of the reds and ambers used for difficulty and fire danger. Font: Public Sans (open licence).

B. Signal orange

Trail-marker orange on granite grey. Louder and easy to spot in sun, but close to the amber, orange and red used for difficulty and fire danger. Font: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (open licence, built for low vision).
