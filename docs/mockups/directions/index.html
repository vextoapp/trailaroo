Design sprint: three directions

The same real trails, photos and map, designed three ways. Open each on your phone and on a laptop. Try the trail page: drag along the elevation profile and watch the dot move along the route.

Pick one, or mix: "A's layout with B's photos and C's first visit" is a perfectly good answer.

A · Field guide

Calm and precise, like Linear and Notion. Map first; the route's magenta, borrowed from printed topo maps, is the brand.

B · Golden hour

Warm and photo-led, like Airbnb and Luma. Every trail is an invitation.

C · Plain and bold

Friendly and direct, like Wise. Big words, three simple questions, chunky controls.
